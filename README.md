## From Applied Linguistics & Instructional Systems to Multi-Agent Orchestration

Earlier in my career, I spent fifteen years in international schools and curriculum leadership across Asia and the US and earned an M.A. in Applied Linguistics from the University of Birmingham. Treating prompts, rubrics, and model outputs as testable linguistic specs led directly into large-scale agentic engineering: governing **52,167 multi-agent `.jsonl` sessions**, wiring **30+ deterministic Python hook gates**, auditing **88,000 API calls** across 12 models, and building automated **SOP-to-SCORM 1.2** compilers before founding **Human Creative**.

Today my work is horizontal across operations, customer support, compliance, and enterprise L&D teams (US Citizen in Seoul, working 100% remotely in English via US W-2 EOR, US LLC B2B contract, or fixed-scope sprints).
